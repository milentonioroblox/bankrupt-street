Bankrupt Street · Street 1 · Greybox plan v2 (large plots)

Skid Row street plan

The map for the first street: 8 plots, the auction stage in the middle, spawn at the west end and the rebirth gate at the east end. All sizes are in Roblox studs and sized for an R15 avatar. Build it as grey blocks first. Art comes after the auction loop is fun.

Street plan (top view)

origin (0, 0) = centre of the plaza · grid 16 studs

Plots are big enough for a 64 × 64 house and eight 18 × 18 property pads, so a Billionaire Estate has room to look rich. The walk from the farthest plot to the stage is about 330 studs, roughly 20 seconds. That is fine because players can bid from anywhere on the street, and the jumbotron is 56 studs wide so it reads from every plot.

One plot (detail)

112 × 128 · road side at the top

North-side plots are mirrored, so the road side always faces the street. Slot pads are numbered 1–8 in the order they unlock. Level 1 (Cardboard Box) uses slots 1–2.

World around the street

top view · 2000 × 2000

The street sits in a valley. Tall derelict buildings directly behind the plots block the view at eye level. Fields and low hills come next, then a ring of mountains that fades into the haze.

Props (Skid Row)

  • Streetlights every 40 studs on both sidewalks, 20 tall, warm light (Range 32).
  • Power poles with sagging wires along both alleys.
  • Dumpsters, trash bags, traffic cones and shopping carts at plot gaps. Keep the slot pads clear.
  • Chain-link fences and graffiti decals (cartoon, no text) on the backdrop buildings.
  • A bus stop and a "Welcome to Skid Row" sign at spawn.
  • A billboard frame on the east backdrop, kept for events such as "MARKET CRASH".
  • Benches and lamp posts around the plaza, facing the stage.

Keep it cartoonish and readable from far away, following the GDD tone rules. No alcohol, drug or crime props.

Build order in Studio

  1. Terrain: flat Ground at Y 0 across the play area, Grass fields around it, and mountain ring last. Generated by script, then hand-sculpted.
  2. Street, sidewalks and plaza as anchored parts, using the coordinates above.
  3. Eight plot models named Plot1–Plot8, each with the attachments the GDD lists: HouseAnchor, Slot1–Slot8, Nameplate, CarPad, HeliPad, plus a ClaimPrompt part at the gate.
  4. Stage, jumbotron (SurfaceGui), rent and crash clock, rescue kiosk and Hall wall.
  5. Spawn area with the daily kiosk, and the rebirth gate (locked).
  6. Backdrop buildings behind both alleys.
  7. Lighting presets. Save, then publish to Roblox as a backup.
  8. Art pass later: ForgeGUI meshes replace the grey blocks one by one without moving any attachments.
